Most standard residential appraisals are completed within 10–14 business days from the inspection date. Rush turnaround (4 business days, +$200) is available for time-sensitive situations like divorce proceedings or estate settlements — select "ASAP" when filling out the form above.

Please ensure access to all interior spaces — all rooms, basement, attic (if accessible), and garage. If you have any of the following available, they can help support a more accurate valuation:

  • Recent renovation receipts or permits
  • HOA documents and fee schedules
  • Prior appraisal reports
  • Survey or plot plan

Appraisal fees vary based on property type, size, location, and complexity. A home inspection identifies physical defects and safety issues in a property. An appraisal is an independent, certified opinion of market value — typically required by lenders and accepted by courts. Both may be needed depending on your situation, but they serve different purposes and are performed by different licensed professionals.
