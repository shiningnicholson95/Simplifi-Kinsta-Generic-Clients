What Is a Residential Home Appraisal?

The Certified Opinion of Value Behind Every Major Real Estate Decision

A residential home appraisal is a certified opinion of a property's fair market value prepared by a licensed appraiser following USPAP standards. It is the foundational document behind virtually every major real estate transaction — required by lenders, courts, the IRS, and government agencies whenever a property's value must be established with legal authority. Unlike an automated estimate or a real estate agent's CMA, a certified appraisal is based on a physical inspection and defensible methodology.

McKoy Harris Valuation provides independent, unbiased residential appraisals for every purpose throughout Chester, Delaware, Montgomery, and Philadelphia counties — with over 500 completed appraisals and deep local market expertise built over years of working exclusively in southeastern Pennsylvania.
