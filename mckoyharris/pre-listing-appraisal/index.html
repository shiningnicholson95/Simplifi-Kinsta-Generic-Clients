What Is a Pre-Listing Appraisal?

Price Your Home Right the First Time — With Certified, Independent Data

A pre-listing appraisal is a certified, USPAP-compliant opinion of your home's fair market value ordered before you put it on the market. Unlike a real estate agent's comparative market analysis — an informal estimate shaped by a desire to win your listing — a pre-listing appraisal is prepared by an independent, licensed appraiser with no stake in the sale price. It's the same report format used by mortgage lenders, courts, and the IRS.

McKoy Harris Valuation helps Chester County sellers price confidently by providing an independent, data-driven valuation before listing day. Whether you're working with an agent, selling FSBO, settling an estate, or navigating a post-divorce sale, a pre-listing appraisal gives you the documented foundation to price strategically — and defend that price when it matters.

What Your Pre-Listing Appraisal Report Covers

Every report is prepared in full compliance with USPAP — the same standard required by mortgage lenders, courts, and the IRS.

  • Complete interior and exterior property inspection
  • USPAP-compliant written appraisal report (Form 1004 or narrative format)
  • Minimum 3 comparable sales with documented adjustments
  • Condition and quality analysis with improvement credit
  • Neighborhood market trend commentary (months of supply, absorption rate)
  • Property sketch and confirmed gross living area
  • Certified appraiser signature and PA license documentation

  • PDF delivery within 24 hours of report completion

How It Works

Your Pre-Listing Appraisal, Step by Step

Submit Your Request

Complete the online request form or call (610) 871-0318. Provide the property address, your anticipated listing timeframe, and any recent improvements you'd like credited. Antwan will follow up within one business day to confirm scope and scheduling.

Pre-Inspection Preparation

Gather documentation for any major improvements — permits, contractor invoices, renovation photos. The more detail you can provide about upgrades and their costs, the more accurate the credit analysis in your report. No documentation is required to schedule.

Property Inspection

Antwan conducts a full interior and exterior inspection — measuring rooms, photographing all spaces, documenting condition, quality, and improvements, and confirming gross living area. The inspection typically takes 45–90 minutes. You're encouraged to walk through and point out upgrades not visible to the eye.

Market Research & Report Preparation

Using current MLS data and deep knowledge of the Chester County and Delaware County markets, Antwan selects the most relevant comparable sales, applies adjustments, and prepares a thorough USPAP-compliant report. Standard turnaround is 10–14 business days.

Report Delivery & Strategy Review

Your completed appraisal is delivered via secure PDF within 24 hours of completion. Antwan is available to walk through the findings with you, answer questions, and discuss how the certified value supports your pricing strategy. Hard copies available on request.

Common Questions About Pre-Listing Appraisals

A pre-listing appraisal is a certified, USPAP-compliant opinion of market value prepared by a licensed appraiser before your home goes on the market. Unlike a real estate agent's comparative market analysis (CMA) — an informal sales tool produced by a party with a stake in your listing — a pre-listing appraisal is independent, unbiased, and based on a physical inspection and documented market analysis. It carries the same legal weight as a mortgage appraisal.

It significantly reduces that risk. One of the most common reasons real estate deals fall apart is an appraisal gap — when the buyer's lender appraises the home below the agreed purchase price, forcing a renegotiation or killing the deal. Pricing your home at a level supported by a certified independent valuation makes a low lender appraisal far less likely. It also gives you documented data to defend your price if negotiations get contentious.

An agent's CMA is a useful starting point, but agents have an inherent interest in winning your listing — which can result in an optimistic estimate. A pre-listing appraisal gives you a second, independent data point. Many sellers use it to evaluate the agent's suggested price, to push back on an agent pricing too conservatively, or to validate a recommended price on a unique property where CMA accuracy is limited. It's a tool, not a replacement for your agent.
