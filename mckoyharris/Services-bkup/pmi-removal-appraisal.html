What Is a PMI Removal Appraisal?

Document Your 20% Equity and End the Monthly PMI Charge

Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI) is required by most conventional lenders when the down payment is less than 20%. Under the federal Homeowners Protection Act, you have the legal right to request PMI cancellation once your loan-to-value ratio reaches 80% — meaning your equity is at least 20% of the home's current market value. A certified appraisal is the documentation your servicer needs to confirm that threshold.

With home values in Chester and Delaware County appreciating significantly over recent years, many homeowners who purchased with less than 20% down have already crossed that threshold — but won't see a dollar of savings without requesting cancellation supported by a certified appraisal. McKoy Harris Valuation delivers a fast, lender-ready report so you can stop overpaying.

How It Works

Your PMI Removal Appraisal, Step by Step

Check Your Lender's Requirements

Before ordering, contact your loan servicer to confirm their PMI cancellation process and appraisal requirements. Some servicers accept independent appraisals directly; others require the appraisal to be ordered through their process. We can advise based on your servicer.

Submit Your Request

Complete the online form or call (610) 871-0318. Provide the property address, your current loan balance (approximate), and your estimated home value. Antwan will follow up within one business day to confirm scope and schedule the inspection.

Property Inspection

Antwan conducts a thorough interior and exterior inspection — photographing all rooms, noting condition and upgrades, and confirming square footage. Having documentation of any recent renovations ready helps capture full value. Typically takes 45–60 minutes.

Market Research & Report Preparation

Using current MLS data, Antwan identifies the most relevant comparable sales, applies appropriate adjustments, and prepares your USPAP-compliant appraisal report. Standard delivery is within 3–5 business days of the inspection.

Submit to Your Servicer

Your completed report is delivered via secure PDF within 24 hours of completion. Submit it to your servicer per their PMI cancellation process. If the value supports 80% LTV or better, your lender can cancel PMI — typically effective on your next billing cycle.
