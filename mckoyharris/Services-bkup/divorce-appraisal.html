What Is a Divorce Appraisal?

An Independent Value That Holds Up in Court

A divorce appraisal is a certified opinion of a property's fair market value, prepared by a licensed appraiser specifically for use in legal separation and divorce proceedings. Unlike an online estimate or a real estate agent's opinion, a certified appraisal is a legally defensible document accepted by courts, attorneys, and mediators throughout Pennsylvania.

Whether you need to determine equitable buyout terms, split marital equity, or establish value as of a specific date of separation, McKoy Harris Valuation delivers an impartial, thoroughly documented report — backed by local market expertise and over 500 completed appraisals across Chester, Delaware, Montgomery, and Philadelphia counties.

When You Need This

Common Situations That Require a Divorce Appraisal

Spouse BuyoutOne spouse is keeping the home and must pay the other their share of the equity at a certified market value.

Court-Ordered ValuationA judge or attorney requires a certified appraisal as part of the equitable distribution process under Pennsylvania law.

Date-of-Separation ValuePennsylvania law may require valuation as of the separation date rather than today. We perform retrospective appraisals for any past effective date.

Settlement NegotiationBoth parties need a neutral, agreed-upon value to anchor equitable distribution discussions and avoid protracted dispute.

Contested DivorceThe two parties disagree on value. Each retains their own appraiser, and the certified reports serve as the evidentiary basis for the hearing.

Multiple PropertiesThe marital estate includes a vacation home, rental property, or investment property — each requiring a separate certified valuation.

How It Works

Your Divorce Appraisal, Step by Step

Submit Your Request

Complete the online request form or call (610) 871-0318. Provide the property address, your appraisal purpose (divorce/settlement), and your preferred timeline. If you have a filing deadline, mention it — rush scheduling is available.

Consultation & Scheduling

Antwan will follow up within one business day to confirm the scope (current vs. retroactive valuation), discuss any special considerations, and schedule the property inspection at a time that works for both parties if needed.

Property Inspection

Antwan conducts a thorough interior and exterior inspection of the subject property — photographing all rooms, noting condition, confirming square footage, and documenting all features relevant to value. Typically takes 45–90 minutes depending on property size.

Market Research & Report Preparation

Using MLS data and local market knowledge, Antwan identifies the most relevant comparable sales, applies appropriate adjustments, and prepares a detailed, USPAP-compliant appraisal report. Standard reports are completed within 3–5 business days.

Report Delivery

Your completed appraisal report is delivered via secure PDF within 24 hours of completion. Hard copies available on request. The report is ready for use by your attorney, mediator, or court — and Antwan is available for expert witness testimony if required.
