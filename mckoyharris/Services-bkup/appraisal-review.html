What Is an Appraisal Review?

An Independent Expert Analysis of an Existing Appraisal Report

An appraisal review is a separate professional service in which a licensed reviewing appraiser analyzes an existing appraisal report for credibility, accuracy, and USPAP compliance. The reviewer evaluates whether the original appraiser's methodology was appropriate, whether the comparable sales were relevant, whether adjustments were reasonable and supported, and whether the conclusions are adequately explained. A review may conclude that the original report is credible — or it may identify specific errors that support a reconsideration or challenge.

McKoy Harris Valuation provides both desk reviews (office-based analysis of the report and market data) and field reviews (which add an exterior inspection of the subject property and comparables). Both are prepared in full compliance with USPAP Standards 3 and 4 and are suitable for submission to lenders, courts, and administrative bodies.
