Cookies

A cookie is a string of information that a website stores on a visitor’s computer, and that the visitor’s browser provides to the website each time the visitor returns. McKoy Harris Valuation uses cookies to help McKoy Harris Valuation identify and track visitors, their usage of McKoy Harris Valuation website, and their website access preferences. McKoy Harris Valuation visitors who do not wish to have cookies placed on their computers should set their browsers to refuse cookies before using McKoy Harris Valuation websites, with the drawback that certain features of McKoy Harris Valuation websites may not function properly without the aid of cookies.
